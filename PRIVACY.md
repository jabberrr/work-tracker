# Diptychon Privacy Policy

*Effective October 20, 2026*

**In short:** Diptychon doesn't collect any of your data. Your data stays on your Mac and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud. There are no analytics, ads, tracking or third-party services.

## Who this covers
This policy covers Diptychon, a macOS app for tracking work sessions, made by Jasper Dabora ("I", "me"). Diptychon has no servers, website accounts or backend of its own.

## Information I collect
**None.** Diptychon doesn't send anything you create, or anything about how you use the app, to me or to anyone else. There are no analytics or advertising tools and no third-party code that collects data, and the app doesn't track you across apps or websites.

## Where your data is kept
Everything you create in Diptychon stays on your devices or in your iCloud. That includes sessions, segments, titles, labels, tags, profiles, notes, learnings, takeaways and images.

- **On your Mac.** Data is saved in Diptychon's private app container, which other apps can't read.
- **In your iCloud, if you turn on sync.** Diptychon uses Apple's CloudKit to keep your Macs in sync through the private database of your Apple Account. Only you can access that database; I can't. Text you write (titles, notes, learnings, takeaways and image captions) is also encrypted with keys from your iCloud Keychain. Apple's [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers how iCloud stores data.
- **Backups and exports.** Automatic backups are saved on your Mac inside Diptychon's container. Exports are saved only where you choose.

## Sign in with Apple
Signing in is optional. If you sign in, Apple gives Diptychon an anonymous user identifier. The first time you sign in, it may also share your name and email (or a private relay address if you choose Hide My Email). Diptychon stores these only in your Mac's Keychain, to show who is signed in. They are never sent to me or to any server.

Signing out removes the identifier from Diptychon. You can also stop using Sign in with Apple for Diptychon in System Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple.

## Diagnostics
Diptychon doesn't collect crash or usage reports. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in macOS, Apple may send me anonymous crash reports. Apple's settings control this, not Diptychon. You can turn it off in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

## Deleting your data
You can delete individual sessions in the app, or everything at once:

- **Settings › Data › Delete All Data** removes every session, label and tag. With iCloud sync on, the deletion syncs to your other Macs and your iCloud.
- Delete Diptychon's backups from the backups list in **Settings › Data**.
- To remove Diptychon's iCloud data, use System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud › Manage.

Since I don't hold any of your data, there is nothing for me to delete on my side.

## Children
Diptychon isn't aimed at children and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy
If Diptychon's data practices ever change, I'll update this page and its effective date before the change ships.
