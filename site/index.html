Getting started

  1. Open Worklog. Sign in with Apple, or choose Continue as Guest. Both work the same, and you can sign in later.
  2. On Today, type what you're working on, pick a label and click Start session. You can also start from the menu bar, the overlay, or with ⇧⌘S.
  3. While you work, add notes (each gets a timestamp) and click Split when you switch to something else.
  4. Click Stop. Give the session a title, add a takeaway for next time and any learnings, then click Save.

Your sessions are in History, your learning points in Learning, and totals and streaks in Stats.

Sessions

I forgot to split when I switched tasks. Can I fix it?

Yes. Open the session in History, then under Segments click Split… and choose the time you switched. A segment's menu also has Merge with Next, Move End Time… and Delete Segment….

How do I change a session's start or end time?

Open it in History and choose Edit Start and End… from the more menu.

How do I add images?

Drag them in, paste them with ⌘V, or click Add images. This works in the live Notes section and in a session's Images section in History. Each image can have a caption. Images can be up to 100 MB each.

What is the takeaway?

A one-line note for your next session. It shows in the overlay and the menu bar until your next session's review. To keep it until you replace it, turn off Settings › General › Show only in the next session.

What do the mastery dots mean?

They show how well you know a learning point, from 1 to 5. Set them when you add the point. The Learning page charts your average mastery over time for each tag.

Does Worklog pause by itself?

It pauses when your Mac sleeps, and when Worklog quits if you turn on Pause when Worklog quits. Both settings are in Settings › General. It never resumes on its own. After 10 hours of running it asks Still working?, and you can change that time in the same place.

What are profiles?

Separate spaces, like Work and Personal, each with its own sessions, stats and labels. Switch profiles at the bottom of the sidebar. A label or tag can be available in all profiles or in one only (Settings › Labels & Tags › Available in).

Sync & backups

How do I sync between my Macs?

Turn on Settings › Account › Sync with iCloud on each Mac and reopen Worklog. Every Mac has to be signed in to the same Apple Account in System Settings. The first sync can take a few minutes.

My sessions aren't showing on my other Mac.

  • Check that both Macs use the same Apple Account and have internet.
  • Open Settings › Account and check that the status says Syncing with iCloud, not Saving to this Mac only.
  • Leave Worklog open for a minute, then check again. A session that's still running shows as Running on another Mac.

What does “Saving to this Mac only” mean?

Worklog is still saving everything, but only on this Mac for now, usually because iCloud isn't available. Click Details… to see why. Your data syncs again once iCloud is available.

Are my sessions backed up?

Yes. Worklog backs up your data on this Mac every hour after changes, and when it quits. It keeps the latest 10 backups. Change the schedule, how many backups to keep and whether to include images in Settings › Data, or click Back Up Now.

How do I restore a backup?

In Settings › Data, find the backup and click Restore…. Choose Merge with current data or Replace current data. Your current data is backed up first, so you can go back.

Can I export my data?

Yes, from Settings › Data › Export: Export JSON… (everything, optionally with images; you can import it again later), Export Sessions CSV… or Export Segments CSV… (for spreadsheets). Exports include all profiles.

How do I delete all my data?

In Settings › Data, click Delete All Data…. With iCloud sync on, this also deletes your data in iCloud and on your other Macs. A backup is saved first; delete it from the backups list if you don't want it. See the Privacy Policy for more.

Account

Do I have to sign in?

No. Continue as Guest works the same. Sync uses the Apple Account your Mac is signed in to, whether or not you sign in to Worklog.

Does signing out delete anything?

No. Signing in doesn't move your data, and signing out deletes nothing. Sign out in Settings › Account.

What does Worklog do with my data?

Nothing. It doesn't collect anything. Your data stays on your Mac and in your own iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.

Customizing

How do I change the look?

In Settings › Appearance, pick a theme (Graphite, Paper or Meadow), light or dark mode, an accent color and a text size.

How do I customize the overlay?

In Settings › Overlay › Layout, click Edit. Drag elements to move them, drag an element's right edge to resize it, and use Add to bring back an element you removed. Click Done when you're finished, or Reset Layout to start over. You can also change opacity, compact mode, keep on top and show on all Spaces there.

Can I hide the Dock icon?

Turn on Settings › General › Hide Dock icon when the window closes. Reopen the window from the menu bar icon.

Keyboard shortcuts

Troubleshooting

The menu bar icon is missing.

Turn on Settings › General › Show in menu bar. If it's on but you still can't see it, your menu bar may be full; quit other menu bar apps or make room in System Settings › Menu Bar.

The overlay doesn't appear.

Turn on Settings › Overlay › Show overlay, or press ⇧⌘O. If Hide when idle is on, it only shows while a session runs.

A shortcut doesn't work.

Shortcuts only work while Worklog is the active app. If another app uses the same keys, record a different combination in Settings › Shortcuts.

Some of my data seems to be missing.

This can happen after changing the Apple Account on your Mac, since each account has its own iCloud data. Switch back to the original account, or restore a recent backup from Settings › Data. If that doesn't help, email me before deleting anything.
